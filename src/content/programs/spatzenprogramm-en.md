## Overview

The **Sparrows Program** is a holistic German-language education program for primary school children with established German language skills. Students are enrolled at Toorak Primary School and fully integrated into the Australian school day – in addition, they receive around **five hours of German instruction per week** at the level of German primary schools.

Our program combines quality German education, cultural connection and bilingual development in a supportive, family-like learning environment.

## Class Options

### Program A
Monday **or** Thursday, 1:00–3:30 pm at Toorak Primary School.

### Program B
Monday **and** Thursday, 3:30–5:00 pm at Toorak Primary School.

### Standard program: A & B

## Concept & Class Structure

- Full integration into the Australian classroom at Toorak Primary School
- German lessons in a separate room, always on **Mondays and Thursdays**
- Approx. 5 hours per week, parallel to and complementing the Australian curriculum
- Lessons taught **exclusively in German** with teaching materials from Germany
- Teachers are German-trained primary school teachers registered in Australia

## Class Groups

| Group | Year levels |
|--------|--------------|
| **Minispatzen** | Prep – Year 2 |
| **Spatzen** | Year 3 – 6 |

Small learning groups allow for individual support.

## Who is it for?

The Sparrows Program is designed for children aged 5–12 who already have **solid German language skills** – usually through a German-speaking family background. Admission follows a short assessment by our teaching team.

**Participation is possible without living in the school zone** – the TPS zoning has never been an obstacle.

## What we teach

Lessons cover all areas of the German language:

- **Speaking** – communication and oral expression
- **Reading** – with a large German children's and youth library
- **Writing** – from cursive handwriting to creative writing
- **Spelling & grammar** – following German primary school textbooks (Baden-Württemberg)

Beyond that, we integrate:
- Music & art
- Science and social topics
- Cultural elements and traditions

## Methods & Teaching Approach

### Immersion – German as the everyday language
Only **German is spoken in the classroom**, creating a natural language environment.

### Mixed-age groups
Strengthen social development, a sense of responsibility and self-confidence.

### Project-based learning
Language learning is embedded in projects – e.g. fairy tale projects, the seasons, exploring cities.

### Holistic learning – "with head, heart and hands"
- Theatre & role play
- Music & movement
- Art & craft projects
- Games, puzzles, discovery

## Benefits of a bilingual school model

Research shows that children raised bilingually have:

- **Stronger cognitive abilities**
- **Better problem-solving skills**
- **Improved attention and learning flexibility**

Our students expand their language skills, deepen their cultural roots and benefit from the Australian school system at the same time. By being integrated into a government school, we can offer quality education at a **moderate cost**.
