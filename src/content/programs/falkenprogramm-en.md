## Who is it for?

The **Falcons Program** is for German-speaking students in **Year 7 to 12** who want to develop their language skills to native speaker level.

## Requirements

**Entry** with:
- Goethe A2, or
- A completed native speaker primary school program (e.g. the Sparrows Program)

Classes are taught **entirely in German**.

## Program Goals

In the Falcons Program, young people deepen their German skills at a high level:

- **Building a very advanced vocabulary**
- Fostering enjoyment of the German language
- Meeting peers who are also native speakers
- **Targeted preparation for the Goethe certificates B1, B2 and C1**
- Building skills that open the door to studying in German-speaking countries
- **Support for the VCE exam** in German (level B1–B2)

## Classes & Content

Lessons include varied reading, writing and language tasks, supported by a modern textbook and digital media:

- Interactive language and grammar exercises
- Text analysis & writing practice
- **Discussion rounds & presentations**
- Listening comprehension, vocabulary and expression exercises
- Weekly homework to consolidate learning

## Time & Place

**Mondays, 5:00–6:30 pm**
Toorak Primary School, Melbourne

The school is within walking distance of:
- **Toorak Station** and **Hawksburn Station**
- **Tram routes 58 and 72**

Parents can use the time for shopping on Toorak Road – or meet for a coffee while the teenagers are in class.
