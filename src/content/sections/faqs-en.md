list:
  - question: "Who is Spatzenschule for?"
    answer: "Spatzenschule is designed for children with solid German language skills – usually from German-speaking families. The Sparrows Program is for Prep to Year 6 (ages 5–12), the Falcons Program for Year 7–12."
  - question: "How does admission work?"
    answer: "Admission follows a short assessment by our teaching team. For the Falcons Program, Goethe A2 or completion of a native speaker primary school program is required."
  - question: "Where do classes take place?"
    answer: "Classes are held at Toorak Primary School – close to Toorak Station, Hawksburn Station and tram routes 58 and 72."
  - question: "When do classes take place?"
    answer: "The Sparrows Program runs on Mondays and/or Thursdays (1:00–3:30 pm or 3:30–5:00 pm). The Falcons Program runs on Mondays from 5:00–6:30 pm."
  - question: "Does my child need to be enrolled at Toorak Primary School?"
    answer: "No, enrolment at Toorak Primary School is not a requirement – the school zoning has never been an obstacle. We welcome children from all over Melbourne."
  - question: "How do I enrol my child?"
    answer: "Download the relevant form from our enrolment page, fill it in and send it to enrolments.spatzenschule@gmail.com. We will get back to you with the next steps."
