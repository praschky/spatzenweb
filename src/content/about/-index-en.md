## Our Story

Spatzenschule Melbourne was founded in **2007** and has grown into one of Melbourne's leading German native speaker programs. Today we support **30–40 students** in the Sparrows and Falcons programs – from Prep to Year 12.

## Our Structure – run by parents, for children

### A not-for-profit parent association

Spatzenschule is run by a registered, not-for-profit parent association – comparable to a German parent initiative. The association is responsible for:

- Employing the teachers
- Managing insurance
- Organising day-to-day operations

### Our Committee

At the operational heart is our **volunteer committee**, elected each year. Its responsibilities include:

- Enrolments & communication
- Finances & administration
- Selecting and supporting our teachers
- Planning cultural activities & school events

## Our Community

All parents automatically become members of the association and are warmly invited to support the program. This creates a **strong, warm and vibrant German-speaking community** that gives our children a stable linguistic and cultural home.

Spatzenschule stands for: ✔️ Bilingual education ✔️ Cultural identity ✔️ Community & engagement ✔️ German as a strong family language
